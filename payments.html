How merchant payouts and 2% BIR withholding tax work Click to read details
2% Creditable Withholding Tax (BIR CWT) Per BIR Revenue Regulations No. 2-98, a 2% withholding tax is deducted from gross sales commissions. BIR Form 2307 certificates are issued quarterly for your income tax crediting.
Milestone Clearance Protocols Disbursements require verified photo documentation, equipment waybills, and field supervisor sign-off before queue release.
Disbursement Schedule Transfers are deposited on the 1st and 15th via PESONet to your verified corporate bank account.
Billing Support Questions regarding calculations? Submit a billing inquiry for account manager assistance.
